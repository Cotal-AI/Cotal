Keys

1 – 4
pick a demo, or press a button inside one
Space
press the highlighted button
R
replay this demo
→ ←
next or previous demo
Esc
back to all demos, then to the idle screen
F
fullscreen
M
sound on or off

Left alone, the screen plays the demos by itself. Any touch hands control back.
