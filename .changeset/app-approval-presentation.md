Report pending permission approval with an authenticated review link and no usable connection. Hosted gateways can resume one unchanged request with its approval ID; the ID grants no authority and cannot rename or replace an approved connection. Native conversations wait for the person's decision before starting their connection or capturing activity. Permission arguments are proposals, never human consent.
