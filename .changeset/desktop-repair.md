The desktop app can now fix this computer from inside the app. The error screens that used to offer only "Try again" or "Check status" now offer Repair where Repair can fix the cause. A screen whose cause nothing in the app can fix, such as a service that belongs to another user account, says why and offers no button. Repair checks the background service, its version, the agent apps it finds and the spaces it runs, shows what it found and what it will change, and runs only after you choose its button. One approval names every change it makes: moving the service from another copy of Cotal, starting a stopped service, and removing an old registration of this computer. When another copy of Cotal runs the background service, Repair moves it to this app. It names the agents that stop and says they don't start again by themselves, and it keeps your sign-in, this computer's identity and every space's records. It never replaces a newer copy, or one whose version it can't tell, with this app; it asks you to update instead. A service whose copy of Cotal can't run where it is, because that copy is gone or sits on a disk image, under App Translocation or in Downloads, is moved to this app whatever its version. It starts only spaces this computer still hosts, never one you disconnected. If this app's service does not start, the move puts the other copy's service back. A step that fails stops Repair, and the panel shows what changed before it, why it stopped and what to do next. A daemon message or next step that names a command or a file path is no longer shown in the window. The same check is `cotal daemon repair` on a computer without the app, where `--yes` approves every step that asks first, and the cross-installation refusal now carries `ANOTHER_INSTALLATION` with the owning copy's kind.
