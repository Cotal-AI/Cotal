The service endpoint host serves `caller-grant`, `caller-revoke`, `caller-policy-read` and `caller-policy-list` instead of answering `unsupported`. A grant needs an interactive owner who holds the approved instance and an interactive grantee with no parent. Its capabilities must be pinned to that instance with `instanceOnly`, carry no journal access and no `any` or `ledger` target, and sit within the approval's ceiling. It is recorded `active` with a host-derived id, source and parents. One instance indexes at most 256 grants. A revoke lands deny-new, then writes the grant's op-bound cutoff marker and a `notBefore` in epoch seconds from the marker's broker time. It answers `pending` and never `revoked`. Each operation record keeps the exact request and the grant's immutable fields, and a positive grant write is recorded before it is issued, so a replay never repeats a write whose outcome is unknown. Grants also need a payload precondition: every server's `max_payload` at least 2 MiB plus 1 KiB, an auth stream with no per-message limit, and an operator assertion recorded in the auth store. Stock records no assertion and its generated broker keeps the 1 MiB default, so a stock deployment answers `unsupported` to grants. A failed check or a revoke write that would not fit withdraws the assertion. The operation kind gains `grant`, operation views accept the caller target, and the result parser accepts the grant and caller-policy read and list results.
