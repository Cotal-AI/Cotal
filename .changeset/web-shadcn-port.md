The web dashboard has a new look built on shadcn components, with the same views and controls. Hover text is a tooltip, the Activity and Agents filters are toggle groups, the theme menu is a dropdown, and deleting a channel asks in an alert dialog. The left side is a sidebar that collapses to its icon rail from the button at the start of each view's header or with Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac); on a phone that button opens the places as a sheet, and the details panel and a message's long-press actions open as sheets too. Channels and direct messages open at the newest message, follow new ones while you are at the bottom, and offer a button to jump back to it. Messages are bubbles with your own on the end side, a run from one sender is grouped, and a long message opens with Show more.
