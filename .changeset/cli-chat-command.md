Add `cotal chat [<channel>]`, a live session under its own identity in the terminal view, as `--plain` lines, or as the `--stdio` NDJSON participant protocol. The command resolves the mesh once and admits the session by that target's recorded auth mode only: a routing identity on an open mesh, a manager-admitted session on a static-auth mesh, and an auth-service session under the signed-in person's `cli` grant on a user-auth mesh. It never falls back to another mode. A named channel is the session's initial channel, and on an authenticated mesh its whole read and post list. Before admission the command reads the channel registry on the resolved mesh through the one-shot connection and read `cotal send` uses, and says whether the channel is registered, unregistered or could not be read; it never creates the channel. `--name` sets the display label, which defaults to the OS login and host with a short suffix. The terminal view refuses without a terminal and names `--plain` and `--stdio`; `--stdio` refuses with `--plain`, and the command declares no `--json`. `--kind endpoint` is accepted and changes nothing, since every admission presents the session as an endpoint; `--kind agent` and any other kind refuse as a usage error. `--role`, `--link`, `--token`, `--creds` and `--lifecycle-uid` are declared and refuse as unsupported until an admission supports them. Signals are held from before admission until the front end handles them, so a session admitted during an interrupt is closed at once and keeps the signal's exit status. A session that ends exits 0 when its close was confirmed, 4 when its retirement was requested and not confirmed, and 3 when the outcome is unknown. Completion offers registered spaces and the target mesh's declared channels. The workspace exports `connectTargetOrThrow`, the connect half of `connectOrThrow` for a target the caller already resolved. The flag inventory and the capability census gain the command's rows and the manager's two chat-session commands.
