A `cotal channel set`, `channel defaults`, `channel remove` (with or without `--purge`) or `history clear` that the broker refuses for the credential's grant now refuses with `PERMISSION_DENIED` (exit 2), naming the credential's profile, the write it lacks and the subject the broker refused, and with `--creds` a Next to run it without `--creds` on the operator credential this machine records. Before, an observer, agent or admin `--creds` got `OPERATION_UNKNOWN` (exit 3) saying the change may have been applied, although the broker had applied nothing. A `channel remove` whose credential may not read the channel registry skips the unknown-name check and lets the broker's answer to the remove decide.
