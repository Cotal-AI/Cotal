Configured macOS desktop builds can install verified app updates while hidden or after Restart to update is pressed. App operations hold replacement back, the shared automatic-update preference is checked again before replacement, and the app relaunches without adding a daemon restart path. The app reports success only after the target background service and ready inventories are observed. Builds without an updater key keep their existing installer and notification controls. Windows and Linux remain installer-only.
