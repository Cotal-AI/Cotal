On OpenCode 2.x, a message that arrives while a turn runs now goes out as the next turn as soon as that turn succeeds, fails or is interrupted. The adapter used to buffer it and wait for an unrelated arrival to wake the seat, so a role request could outlive its ack wait and be handed to another holder of the role. A boot prompt held back by a running turn goes out at the turn end the same way. When a turn ends before OpenCode answers the request that submitted it, the adapter waits for that answer before it submits again, so the boot prompt or a run turn is not sent twice. A failed turn's own batch is not resubmitted at once, so a turn that keeps failing does not loop. A turn-start or turn-end event that the session's durable event log places at or before the end of the last turn is ignored, as is a turn end with no turn running, so a replayed turn does not hold the seat busy, settle the next turn's messages before that turn runs, or resubmit a failed batch. A turn-start or turn-end event without a log position is ignored and logged, since it cannot be told from a replay.
