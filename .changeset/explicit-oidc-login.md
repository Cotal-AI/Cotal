Sign in to compatible standards-based OIDC providers with `cotal login --oidc`. Cotal uses device approval, stores rotating refresh credentials in the selected sign-in store, and exchanges access tokens for short-lived mesh proofs. First startup can pin the provider's audience and JWKS URL. The default Better Auth flow and fresh-human proof policy are unchanged.
