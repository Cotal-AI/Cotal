`cotal auth mint` refuses a label or `--role` its grammar does not take by naming the character, the value and the characters allowed, with a Next that names a value it takes, such as `p_agent` for `p-agent`. Before, every such refusal said only "use a valid credential label and role without path or terminal controls" and printed the command's help. The grammar is unchanged.
