Add the dormant service host operations behind the service custody writer: register admission, approve, refuse, revoke and the operation and proposal reads, over closed per-key records in the auth store. Each invocation writes only the keys its discovery located, and holds before any other write. Its effect connection has its own credential, which publishes only those keys and expires at the invocation's original deadline, rounded up to the whole second, with no renewal or reconnect. The index does not export them. An authority plane or hosted auth service opened with `serviceEndpoints` (off by default, no CLI flag) hosts them and gains an in-process `serviceEndpointHost` for decide, revoke and the two reads; register admission stays internal, so nothing in the product creates a proposal, and the embedder must authenticate the granter it passes. The host shares the plane's claim hold, account and actor ledger, refuses a restore or missing startup, reads through an ordinary renewing read-only discovery connection, trips on both plane fatal paths, and on close waits for every call that entered and closes again every effect connection that did not close, failing with the claim still held while one remains, before the claim is released. The service host contract gains the typed results and closed parsers these operations return. Once an operation is bound, `pending`, `held` and `refused` results name it, revoke results report whether deny-new landed when they have evidence for it (another assignment's endpoint gate is `unknown` on every path that reads the gate), and completed results say whether they were applied now or returned from the record. Proposal and operation reads gain separate terminal views that carry no commands or cluster digests.
