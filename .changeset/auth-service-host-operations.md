Add the dormant service host operations behind the service custody writer: register admission, approve, refuse, revoke and the operation and proposal reads, over closed per-key records in the auth store. Each invocation writes only the keys its discovery located, and holds before any other write. Its effect connection has its own credential, which publishes only those keys and expires at the invocation's original deadline, rounded up to the whole second, with no renewal or reconnect. Nothing in the product constructs them yet and the index does not export them. The service host contract gains the typed results and closed parsers these operations return. Once an operation is bound, `pending`, `held` and `refused` results name it, revoke results report whether deny-new landed when they have evidence for it (another assignment's endpoint gate is `unknown` on every path that reads the gate), and completed results say whether they were applied now or returned from the record. Proposal and operation reads gain separate terminal views that carry no commands or cluster digests.
