The service door admits the schema closures a registration's commands reference. A registration may submit, with its cluster closures, the closure of each `inputDigest` and `outputDigest` its commands name; a submitted one is admitted only whole (its manifest at that digest, its root and every member present, and the bundle recompiling to that closure digest) and is published with the cluster artifacts, so a service registering through the door publishes its schemas the way an in-process registrant does. A registration that submits only its cluster closures is admitted as before and its persisted body and replay are unchanged. A hosted context with service endpoints publishes the canonical void closure (manifest and schema) create-only when it opens, under an effect scoped to exactly those two artifacts, so a payload-free command resolves against it whether or not its registration carried it. `@cotal-ai/core` exports `parseClosureManifest`.
