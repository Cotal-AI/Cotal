Desktop sign-in continues when a space name is already registered and shows which registration was kept and how to replace it. The existing registration stays in place unless you choose to remove it.

Overlapping refreshes on the same daemon share the complete list of conflicts. Desktop keeps large conflict lists intact and does not suggest removing a local registration when its catalog replacement is unavailable.
