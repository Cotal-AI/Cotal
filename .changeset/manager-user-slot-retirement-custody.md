Every managed user-mode lifecycle a manager provisions now records durable slot custody under `mgrslot.<holderOwner>.<targetOwner>-<actor>` (plus its holder index `mgrslot.<holderOwner>._index` and local `mgrslots.json` written before the KV write) in `KV_cotal_records_<space>` and reaches terminal retirement when its seat is proved gone. `remoteManagerPermissions` grants the supervisor credential only subject-confined publish and `DIRECT.GET` access on `mgrslot.<holderOwner>.*`. `enrollUserAgent` records `phase: "enrolling"` with `tokenHash` and `enrollSpec` before calling `enrollManagedAgent` so a lost enrollment reply or crash converges on `tokenHash` and retires the unlaunched lifecycle on startup; `/manager-service-authority` HTTP 403 refusals carry `code: EpErrorCode`, `enrollManagedAgent` raises `code: "already-exists"` exclusively when the alias is enrolled under another `tokenHash` (mapping an activation-saga `already-exists` on an unretired head to `failed-precondition`), and startup convergence retires an `enrolling` row at `provisionalUid` only on `already-exists` while keeping `conflict` (stale serve epoch), `failed-precondition`, and transient failures in `enrolling` for retry. `inspectProcessStart`, `identityVerdict`, `reapSeat`, `drainSeats`, and `probeRecordedProcess` use a three-valued fail-closed contract (`"live" | "gone" | "unknown"`) so an unreadable `/proc/<pid>/stat` (`EACCES`) keeps custody rather than claiming exit. Startup reconciliation preserves live released custodial seats (with bounded `unknown` re-probes and exponential retirement retry backoff), enforces `keepCustody` at `driveDeprovision` as the single custody gate, defers and adopts resumed inventories across `resumePreserved` -> `finalizeResume`, and leaves cross-owner delegated launches (`SPEC §13.16`) held until a user retirement intent confirms retirement.

Compatibility: `@cotal-ai/seat` widens `identityVerdict`'s return type from `"live" | "gone"` to `"live" | "gone" | "unknown"`, throws on `unknown` liveness in `reapSeat` and `drainSeats` instead of treating unreadable `/proc` state as exit, and adds `inspectProcessStart`, `captureProcessIdentity`, `probeRecordedProcess`, and their types; `@cotal-ai/core` adds subject-confined `$KV.<REC>.mgrslot.<owner>.*` and `$JS.API.DIRECT.GET.KV_<REC>.$KV.<REC>.mgrslot.<owner>.*` publish rules for supervisor actors in `remoteManagerPermissions`; `@cotal-ai/auth` adds `code: EpErrorCode` to `/manager-service-authority` HTTP 403 JSON responses, raises `already-exists` (instead of `conflict`) when `enrollManagedAgent` refuses a conflicting `tokenHash` while mapping an activation-saga `already-exists` on an unretired head to `failed-precondition`, and surfaces `EpEnvelopeError` from `managerAuthorityRefusal` when `code` is in `EP_ERROR_CODES`; and `@cotal-ai/manager` adds `userReconciliation` to `ManagerStatus` and holds the user-mode spawn gate closed while startup slot reconciliation runs or if the slot scan fails.
