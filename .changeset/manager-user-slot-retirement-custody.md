Every managed user-mode lifecycle a manager provisions now records durable slot custody under `mgrslot.<holderOwner>.<targetOwner>-<actor>` (plus its holder index `mgrslot.<holderOwner>._index` and local `mgrslots.json` written before the KV write) in `KV_cotal_records_<space>` and reaches terminal retirement when its seat is proved gone. `remoteManagerPermissions` grants the supervisor credential only subject-confined publish and `DIRECT.GET` access on `mgrslot.<holderOwner>.*` (never a records-stream consumer). `enrollUserAgent` records `phase: "enrolling"` with `tokenHash` and `enrollSpec` before calling `enrollManagedAgent` so a lost enrollment reply or crash converges on `tokenHash` and retires the unlaunched lifecycle on startup; `/manager-service-authority` HTTP 403 refusals carry `code: EpErrorCode`, `enrollManagedAgent` raises `code: "already-exists"` when the alias is enrolled under another `tokenHash`, and startup convergence retires an `enrolling` row at `provisionalUid` only on `already-exists` while keeping `conflict` (stale serve epoch) and transient failures in `enrolling` for retry. `inspectProcessStart`, `identityVerdict`, `reapSeat`, `drainSeats`, and `probeRecordedProcess` use a three-valued fail-closed contract (`"live" | "gone" | "unknown"`) so an unreadable `/proc/<pid>/stat` (`EACCES`) keeps custody rather than claiming exit. Startup reconciliation preserves live released custodial seats (with bounded `unknown` re-probes and exponential retirement retry backoff), enforces `keepCustody` at `driveDeprovision` as the single custody gate, defers and adopts resumed inventories across `resumePreserved` -> `finalizeResume`, and leaves cross-owner delegated launches (`SPEC §13.16`) held until a user retirement intent confirms retirement.

Compatibility: `@cotal-ai/seat` changes `inspectProcessStart`, `identityVerdict`, `processGroupOf`, and `groupMembers` to return three-valued fail-closed results (`"live" | "gone" | "unknown"`) and throws on `unknown` liveness in `reapSeat`/`drainSeats` instead of treating unreadable `/proc` state as exit; `@cotal-ai/core` replaces any `KV_cotal_records_<space>` consumer grant in `remoteManagerPermissions` with subject-confined `mgrslot.<owner>.*` publish and `DIRECT.GET` rules; `@cotal-ai/auth` adds `code: EpErrorCode` to `/manager-service-authority` HTTP 403 JSON responses, raises `already-exists` (instead of `conflict`) when `enrollManagedAgent` refuses a conflicting `tokenHash`, and surfaces `EpEnvelopeError` from `managerAuthorityRefusal` when `code` is present; and `@cotal-ai/manager` holds the user-mode spawn gate closed while startup slot reconciliation runs or if the slot scan fails.
