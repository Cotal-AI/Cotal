Make the coordination tools say what to do next. The results of `cotal_send`, `cotal_dm` and `cotal_spawn` now say where the post, message or seat went and the next move. A post says the agents on the channel see it, and a post to a new name says so and names close matches. A DM says other agents can't read it and points anything others could use at the task's channel. A started seat names its `join` channels, or says it starts only on the channels its persona lists. The descriptions of `cotal_send`, `cotal_dm`, `cotal_anycast`, `cotal_spawn`, `cotal_persona`, `cotal_despawn`, `cotal_join`, `cotal_leave`, `cotal_channels`, `cotal_channel_info` and `cotal_run` say what each tool is for, when a neighbouring tool fits better, and give one example, and the briefing's channel guidance agrees with them. The briefing and `cotal_dm` both say to post a status meant for one peer on the shared channel with a mention, and to keep DMs for what that peer alone needs. The briefing and `cotal_send` say a post on a live channel misses an agent that is disconnected or busy, and the briefing and `cotal_spawn` say `once` works on Claude Code, jcode, OpenCode and pi helpers. A persona that would not load is refused as `Couldn't define <name>: it would not load: <reason>`. `MeshAgent.describeSendChannel` returns `{ known, hints }` instead of a sentence.
