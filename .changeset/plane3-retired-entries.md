The Plane-3 trusted reader now removes a durable inbox entry addressed to a retired lifecycle the first time it meets one. Retirement purges the lifecycle's read-ACL row and leaves a tombstone, so the reader drops the entry and deletes it from the inbox stream at once, without ten redeliveries and a give-up line. Before this, every such entry was given up and kept, so any reader that started from a fresh cursor swept the whole backlog again. An entry whose ACL row is simply missing is still retried, given up and kept, because a missing row does not prove the owner is gone. The reader acks a retired entry only after its delete succeeds, so a failed delete is retried, up to the same ten redeliveries, instead of leaving the entry stored with nothing left to remove it. An entry the stream no longer holds counts as removed. A daemon that stops serving while that delete is in flight leaves the entry pending for the daemon that serves next, instead of giving it up. The `delivery` credential gains `STREAM.MSG.DELETE` on the inbox stream for this.
