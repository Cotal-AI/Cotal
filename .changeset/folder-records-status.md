In a copy or a moved project folder, with another space selected, `cotal status` marked the selection in its Spaces list, then headed a `Selected space` section over the folder's records of another space, and printed no `Mesh:` line, while `ps` and `send` there refused under `Mesh: <space>`. It now reports the space the folder's records name, as those commands do: under its `Mesh:` heading and in `target.space`, marked in the Spaces list, in a `This folder` section that says what the folder holds, with the step those commands name under Next. `--json` also names the recorded folder as `selected.recordedRoot`. Bare `cotal` there named nothing the folder holds and went straight to Usage. It now says on one line what the folder holds, with that step: a copy of a controller stack, a legacy root's records and the folder that space is registered for, or records moved from their recorded folder. A refused `cotal up --server <url>` from a running legacy project's subfolder printed `Mesh: <space>` on stdout as the receipt heading and again on stderr with the refusal. The space is now checked before the receipt starts, so the heading prints once, with the refusal on stderr. `--json` is unchanged. `@cotal-ai/workspace` gains `recordsElsewhereText`.
