The README's "How it works" section shows the multicast, unicast and anycast cards at a fixed 270 px in one centred row, with their three lines underneath. On GitHub the three cards share a row from a 1280 px window up instead of stacking at the full column width; a narrower desktop window wraps them to two and one, and a phone shows one per row. On a screen 360 px or wider every card keeps its smallest text above 10 CSS px. The docs pages what-is-cotal and presence-and-delivery keep the cards stacked: the docs site's content column is at most 720 px, too narrow for three cards whose text stays readable. No published package changes.
