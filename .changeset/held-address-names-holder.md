A controller `up` whose broker address another broker already answers now says where to go next. The refusal said only "something already answers at the stack's broker address", without the address, and its next step was to inspect the stack's members. It now names the address and any space this machine registered there. For a first start, the next step is `cotal up --server` at another port, or stopping that space and running `cotal up` again. A stack that ran keeps its recorded address, so the next step is stopping that space, then a bare `cotal up`.
