WHY EVERY REFUSAL GOT TWO. One mutation proves the assertion arm only. Removing a guard shows the
cell notices its absence; making the guard UNCONDITIONAL shows the cell notices its over-firing —
and the second is what proves the control arm was never vacuous. MX17 is why this is not
ceremony: it SURVIVED, with the gate confirmed, because the cell asserted a TAG that a second
production site also emits.

THE CLASSIFIER PAIR IS ONE EXPERIMENT, NOT TWO. `credential-source-unavailable` and
`broker-unreachable` come out of the same function, and it exists to keep them apart — a launcher
fault and a dead host send an operator to different systems. MX19 deletes the credential-source
branch (D6a reddens; the dial failure falls through to broker-unreachable). MX20 makes that branch
unconditional (D6c reddens; a dead port is reported as a launcher fault). Each mutation reddens the
OTHER refusal's cell, which is what "discriminating pair" has to mean to be worth the words.
